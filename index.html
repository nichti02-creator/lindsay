The microphone is turned off for this page

That's OK! Here's how to turn it on, then tap Record again.

On an iPhone (Safari)

  1. Tap the aA button (or the small page icon) at the left of the web address.
  2. Tap Website Settings.
  3. Set Microphone to Allow, then tap Done.
  4. Reload the page and tap Record.

Still blocked? Open the Settings app → Apps → Safari (older iPhones: Settings → Safari) → Microphone → Ask or Allow.

On an Android phone (Chrome)

  1. Tap the icon at the left of the web address (it looks like a lock or two sliders).
  2. Tap Permissions (or Site settings).
  3. Turn Microphone on / set it to Allow.
  4. Reload the page and tap Record.

Still blocked? Open Settings → Apps → Chrome → Permissions → Microphone → Allow.

Opened this from Facebook, Instagram or another app? Tap the ••• menu and choose Open in Safari or Open in Chrome. Or use “Upload a voice memo instead” below.
